Limitations & Discussion
Acknowledged Limitations
01
Temporal Drift
The dataset is historical. As phishing tactics evolve, the model requires periodic retraining on fresh data to maintain accuracy in a production environment.
02
Lexical-Only Scope
Sophisticated domain-spoofing attacks (e.g. paypal.com.verify-login.net) may score lower than expected. Content-based features would improve detection of these cases.
03
Dataset Representativeness
The Kaggle dataset may not fully represent current phishing campaigns, particularly mobile-targeted attacks, homograph attacks, or newly emerging URL obfuscation patterns.
Contributions to Literature
C1
Largest Controlled Comparison
Empirical comparison of three classifiers on 641,053 URLs — one of the largest purely lexical datasets in reviewed literature, with a standardised evaluation protocol.
C2
Explicit FPR Reporting
False Positive Rate reported for all three models — a metric critical for user experience yet absent from most published studies. RF achieved 5.45% FPR, meeting the <10% non-functional requirement.
C3
Open-Source Reproducible Pipeline
Complete codebase on GitHub with fixed random seeds, modular scripts, and a working web prototype — enabling full replication and extension by future researchers.
