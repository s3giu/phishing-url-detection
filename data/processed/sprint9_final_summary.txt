INTERPRETATION
  Random Forest significantly outperforms the linear models
  (LR and SVM) on all metrics, confirming that the relationship
  between lexical URL features and maliciousness is non-linear.
  The high AUC (>0.98) demonstrates strong discriminative power.
  The FPR is the key usability metric: a lower FPR means fewer
  legitimate URLs are incorrectly blocked, which is critical for
  real-time, client-side deployment.
